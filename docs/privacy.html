Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026 · Effective Immediately
Core Privacy Commitment: VOX is architected from the ground up as a 100% local-first desktop application. Your microphone audio, system audio recordings, meeting transcripts, and raw notes never leave your personal computer.

1. Data That Never Leaves Your PC

Unlike cloud dictation tools that stream your live audio to external servers, VOX executes speech recognition and summarization directly on your machine using local neural networks:

  • Microphone & System Audio: Processed entirely in RAM and local WAV files. No audio bytes are ever sent to VOX servers or third-party cloud APIs.
  • Transcripts & Notes: Stored in your local SQLite database at %APPDATA%/VoxLocal/data/voxlocal.db.
  • Meeting Intelligence & LLM MoM: Executed via local offline models (Llama 3.2 1B Instruct) without external network dependency.

2. Commercial Metering & Account Data

To support Free and Pro subscription entitlements, the installed application communicates with our serverless billing ledger:

  • Numerical Usage Aggregates: Weekly committed word count, meeting audio seconds, and Ask VOX query count. These aggregates contain zero conversational text.
  • Account Profile: Your Google account email, name, and profile picture (when signed in) used solely for subscription association.
  • Entitlement Tokens: Cryptographically signed JWT tokens stored locally in Windows DPAPI encrypted vaults.

3. Payment Processing

All subscription payments are securely processed by Razorpay, our certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. VOX does not collect or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials on our servers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have 100% ownership and control over your local data. You may delete any recording, transcript, or your entire database directly through the VOX Settings > Data and Privacy panel at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact our privacy team at privacy@voxlocal.ai.
