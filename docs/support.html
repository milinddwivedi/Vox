Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does VOX require an active internet connection to work?
No. All speech recognition and meeting summarization run locally on your PC. An internet connection is only needed once every few days to verify your subscription or sync numerical usage aggregates.

Q: How do I change my voice typing hotkey?
Open VOX Settings > General > Global Hotkeys. You can assign any key or shortcut like F8, CapsLock, Right Alt, or Ctrl+Win.

Q: Where are my meeting recordings saved?
Recordings and transcripts are saved in your local directory at %APPDATA%/VoxLocal/data/recordings.
